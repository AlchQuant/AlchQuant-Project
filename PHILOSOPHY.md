# Philosophy

AlchQuant is an autonomous quantitative research and execution system
designed to discover, measure, and validate market edge.

This document states the purpose, principles, and objectives of the
program. It describes why the work is done the way it is done, not
how the system is implemented.

## Purpose

Markets reward edge, and edge must be earned, not assumed. AlchQuant
exists to find out, with evidence, whether an autonomous system can
discover a real edge, measure it honestly, and validate it before
anything is risked on it. The program works on newly launched tokens,
where edge is hardest to prove and easiest to imagine.

The program is therefore research first and execution second. A
system that cannot show its edge in controlled conditions has no
business trading in live ones.

## Principles

### Evidence before exposure

No version trades with real funds until it has been demonstrated in
paper and shadow runs. A failed check blocks the version. Doubt is
resolved by not proceeding.

### Independent review

Each design version is reviewed by independent reviewers before
further development proceeds. Review is not a formality; it is the
mechanism by which the program corrects itself.

### Decisions are recorded

Every design decision is recorded, examined, and resolved
individually. The decision record is part of the design, not an
afterthought: a system whose reasoning cannot be followed cannot be
trusted.

### Merit over metrics

One sound result is worth more than many shallow ones. The program
measures itself by the correctness of its conclusions, not by the
volume of its output.

### Build a system, do not rush

The work is the construction of a system, not a race to a result.
Speed that skips validation is not progress; it is debt that comes
due in live trading.

### Open process, guarded implementation

The process is public; the implementation is not. Progress,
decisions, failures, and lessons are shared openly. The source code,
the models, and the decision logic are not. Open development is a
commitment to transparency about the work, not a license to reuse it.

## Objectives

- Build an autonomous system whose edge is demonstrated, measured,
  and validated — not asserted.
- Keep a complete public record of the research: what was tried,
  what failed, what was learned, and why each decision was made.
- Reach a first paper run only when the readiness gate is met, and
  live trading only when the evidence warrants it.

## What this is not

AlchQuant is not a product, not a service, and not investment advice.
Nothing in this repository is a trading signal, and nothing here
authorizes the use or reuse of the system.
